# 13 — Customer Delivery

## Discovery
### Business
Use cases, criticality and acceptable autonomy.

### Inventory
Platforms, number of agents, owners, environments and shadow agents.

### Identity
Interactive/autonomous, resources, permissions, credentials and revocation.

### Data
Sources, classification, regulated data, DLP, residency and retention.

### Tools
Tools/MCP, read/write, reversibility, third parties and secrets.

### Security
SOC ownership, Defender, detections, incident process and evidence.

## Suggested 2–4 week POC
1. Baseline: inventory + gap analysis.
2. Controls: identity/governance/Defender/Purview.
3. Integration: tools/MCP/observability/custom agent.
4. Operate: runbook/dashboard/review/roadmap.

## Exit criteria
Reliable inventory, ownership, one governed end-to-end flow, validated identity model, security/data evidence, incident path and prioritized backlog.

## 30/60/90
- 30 days: visibility and ownership.
- 60 days: identity/security/data baseline.
- 90 days: operating model, automation, scale-out and KPIs.
